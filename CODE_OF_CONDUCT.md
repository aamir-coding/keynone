# Code of conduct

Keynone follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good intent, critique ideas rather than
people, and leave out harassment of any kind. Maintainers may remove comments,
issues or contributions that don't, and may block people who keep at it.

To report a problem privately, contact the maintainers through GitHub (for
example the private reporting described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).
